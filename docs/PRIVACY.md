# PingPlanet privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

PingPlanet is a free, open source network speed test. This policy covers the PingPlanet Android app.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it does not send your data or test results to the developer.
The developer has no server that receives anything from the app.

## What other parties can see

- **Internet test.** When you start this test, the app connects to public test servers run by
  [Vultr](https://www.vultr.com), [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com), [OVH](https://www.ovhcloud.com) and
  [Cloudflare](https://speed.cloudflare.com), and downloads test files from them. Like any website, these companies
  can see your IP address and that your device requested the file. Their own privacy policies apply.
- **LAN test.** The app connects only to the address you type in.
- **Server tab.** While it is running, any device that can reach your phone on the chosen port can run a speed test
  against it and see your phone's IP address.

## What stays on your device

The app remembers the last LAN address you typed, so you don't need to type it again. Test results are kept only in
memory while the app is open. If you tap **Share report**, you choose which app receives the report and what happens
to it after that is up to you. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Mobile data

The download test can use a few hundred MB per server on a fast link. On a metered connection the app starts with
downloads off and asks before using them.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** to run the tests.
- **Network state:** to tell whether the connection is metered.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, files, camera or microphone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
